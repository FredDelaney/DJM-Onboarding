# DJM Career

The player is not operating a CRM. The experience answers: what matters now, what should I do, how am I progressing and is DJM taking care of me?

Core areas are My Week, Development, Season, Evidence, Career, Decisions, Vault and My Team. Verified facts remain visually distinct from player reflection. Preparation readiness is never football ability.

Player-visible service events are neutral: career review completed, evidence reviewed, document verified, information requested or plan updated. Market activity, club identity and negotiation remain internal unless deliberately disclosed.
