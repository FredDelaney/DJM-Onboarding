# DJM Intelligence

DJM Intelligence is the private operating intelligence layer for DJM Sports Management. It should improve every time DJM captures an interaction, verifies evidence, makes a decision and records an outcome.

## Products

- **Command:** what deserves attention, why and whether to act or hold
- **Career:** a player's private weekly, development, evidence and career companion
- **Decision:** one player + one club/opportunity + one intentional approved share
- **Brain:** permission-aware access to structured facts, chronology, relationships and sourced knowledge

## Flywheel

Interaction → evidence → structured knowledge → trusted intelligence → opportunity → recommendation → human decision → execution → outcome → learning.

## Non-negotiables

- No talent score, fake probability, invented value or unsupported fact
- Hard constraints beat semantic similarity
- Human approval for external communication, disclosure, publishing, contractual truth and money
- AI failure never breaks core CRM, Career, Decision or Deals workflows
- Every material answer should expose evidence, truth state, freshness and uncertainty
