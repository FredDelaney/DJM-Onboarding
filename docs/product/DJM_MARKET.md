# DJM Market

Market contains clubs, people, relationship routes, confirmed requirements, historical demand and emerging sourced signals.

Requirements preserve original source context, hard constraints, soft preferences, budget basis, timing, confidence, truth state and expiry. Matching evaluates hard blockers first, then evidence-backed context. Results show strengths, concerns, blockers, missing information and a suggested action—not an opaque player rank.

Every live requirement exposes a complete manual-search brief: position, age band, preferred foot, transfer route, transfer-fee budget, salary budget and period, original request text, registration constraints, expiry and unresolved search gaps. Staff can copy that brief for use in scouting platforms, messages and external search while the same structured fields continue to drive DJM matching.
